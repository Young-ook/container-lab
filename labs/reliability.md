# Reliability
Reliability engineering is a sub-discipline of systems engineering that emphasizes the ability of equipment to function without failure. Reliability is defined as the probability that a product, system, or service will perform its intended function adequately for a specified period of time; or will operate in a defined environment without failure. So, many people think that is stability of services, but, actually, in statistics and psychometrics, reliability is the overall consistency of a measure. A measure is said to have a high reliability if it produces similar results under consistent conditions. Strictly speaking, reliability engineering is about ensuring that a service delivers the expected results at the exact moment the customer expects them—whenever that may be.

Reliability is closely related to availability, which is typically described as the ability of a component or system to function at a specified moment or interval of time.

## Load Testing
As explained, reliability refers to the process of verifying and measuring a system’s ability to consistently produce expected results. Load testing is one of several validation processes; it is a procedure designed to demonstrate that a service can handle the expected volume of requests.
